Texas State University Championship-Caliber Women’s Gymnastics Program Launch

Prepared for Texas State senior leadership as a recommendation from the Women’s Collegiate Gymnastics Association and the Collegiate Gymnastics Growth Initiative.

Texas State has a first-mover opportunity in the most talent-rich state in the country. This roadmap shows the funding sequence required to convert that opportunity into a sustainable Division I launch: invest early in the head coach and first assistant, fund portal-driven recruiting before the first competition cycle, and phase facilities in a way that supports roster construction instead of delaying it.

Decision 1

Hire the head coach first, then bring in the head assistant early enough to build real recruiting momentum before the inaugural roster is locked.

Decision 2

Fund recruiting above a normal startup level because the opening roster needs transfer portal athletes to balance class years and compete faster.

Decision 3

Phase the facility investment without letting construction sequencing crowd out the coaching and roster moves that determine whether year one is credible.

The Funding Sequence Matters More Than The Total

This roadmap is built to answer the executive question directly: what must be funded first, what can phase in later, and what sequence gives Texas State the best chance to launch with real competitive credibility.
